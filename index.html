Let’s See What the Machine Can Do!

A system under test, its requirements and the Test Case Generator, live.

System under test

Meet the system under test: Airport Stand Allocation Simulator ✈️

For the demo, we pretend this simulator is a real system going through UAT. Arriving aircraft are automatically given the smallest suitable available stand based on their wingspan. If no stand fits, they wait in the ground waiting area until one is released.

The plan: take the requirements for this system, feed them into the Test Case Generator, and see which test cases come out. Then we review them, like testers.

Business rules · UAT

Requirements for the system under test

A selection of the functional UAT requirements. These are what we feed into the Test Case Generator.

  1. BR-001

    Smallest Suitable Available Stand

    When an aircraft arrives, the system must assign the smallest available stand capable of accommodating its wingspan: stand.maxWingspan >= aircraft.wingspan. Among all eligible available stands, select the one with the lowest maximum wingspan. If the preferred stand is unavailable, select the next smallest suitable available stand.

    Examples: 20 m → A1 35 m → A2 50 m → A3 65 m → A4 (if available)

    A 35 m aircraft must be assigned to A3 if A2 is unavailable and A3 is available. Allocation must always be calculated from actual stand availability and aircraft wingspan.

  2. BR-002

    Exclusive Stand Allocation

    Only one aircraft may reserve or occupy a stand at any time.

  3. BR-003

    Ground Waiting Queue

    If no suitable stand is available, the aircraft must taxi to a dedicated ground waiting area.

  4. BR-004

    Waiting Queue Reallocation

    Whenever a stand becomes available, the system must reevaluate waiting aircraft. Prioritize waiting aircraft by arrival order, while allowing a later aircraft to use a stand that an earlier aircraft cannot physically accommodate. When multiple stands are available, apply the smallest-suitable-stand rule. Queue allocation must be deterministic and must not create duplicate reservations.

  5. BR-005

    Stand Release

    A stand becomes available only when the departing aircraft has physically cleared the stand.

  6. BR-006

    Oversized Aircraft

    Aircraft with wingspans greater than 70 m must not receive a parking stand. The system should identify such aircraft as unsupported rather than placing them indefinitely in the waiting queue.
